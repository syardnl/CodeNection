Impact

Creativity / Novelty

15%
  • Mirror Pet — no feed/water loop, pet is the burnout data, not a gamified add-on
  • Explicitly rejected streaks/badges — novelty bar was "would a judge have seen this exact idea before?"

Feasibility

15%

Presentation

15%
