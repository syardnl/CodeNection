Why this loop matters for the pitch
This is the one screen sequence that proves "capacity, not time" in front of judges: same Amir, same day, but the plan visibly changes because his capacity changed — not because a deadline moved. Nothing here runs silently: the recheck-in is a button Amir presses, every AI extraction point pauses for confirm/adjust, and every recommendation is accept/adjust/keep-anyway. That's the consistency thread running through all three lanes.
